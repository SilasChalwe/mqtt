Understanding Power vs Energy
------------------------------

POWER = Rate of energy consumption/generation (measured in WATTS)
  Formula: P = V × I (Voltage × Current)
  
ENERGY = Total power consumed/generated over time (measured in WATT-HOURS or Wh)
  Formula: E = P × t (Power × Time)

At 12V system voltage:
  1 Amp continuous = 12 Watts
  1 Ampere-hour (Ah) = 12 Watt-hours (Wh)
